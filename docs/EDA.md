## Heading-like lines

| Kind | Candidate lines | Kept by markers.heading_kind | Files with one kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| caps | 4,530 | 1,286 | 692 |
| numbered | 1,105 | 267 | 112 |
| underlined | 691 | 5 | 5 |

Candidates are lines of 60 characters or less that are not From:/To:/Sent:/cc:/Subject:/Date:/Bcc: lines: ALL CAPS lines and numbered lines that stand alone between blank lines, and any line with a ---- or ==== line right below. Most candidates are noise, so `src/markers.py` adds filters:

- nothing ending in `.` `?` `!` `;` `,` and nothing with `<<`, `http`, `@` or `/` (sentences, attachment names, links, addresses, itinerary names like `MANN/C KAY`)
- ALL CAPS: 4+ letters, starts with a letter, at least half letters, no inner `:`, and 2+ words or one word of 6+ letters. This drops `FYI` (284 lines), initials like `JWVS`, `[IMAGE]`, `- FILE.DOC` and `TIME: 2:00 PM`.
- numbered: the title after the number starts with a capital, is Title Case (every word of 4+ letters capitalized) and has 8 words or less, so `3. The agreement reference in 6.7.1` is a list item, not a title
- underlined: blank line above, underline about as long as the text, Title Case, 8 words or less. Most raw candidates are sentences sitting above a separator line.

- kept caps: `FOR ASSISTANCE` (42), `SCHEDULED SYSTEM OUTAGES:` (41), `W E E K E N D   S Y S T E M S   A V A I L A B I L I T Y` (40), `E-MAIL NOTICE` (37), `CONFIDENTIAL` (31), `FERC DOCUMENTS` (24), `DRAFT DRAFT DRAFT` (20), `MV SALVADOR CP 11.05.00` (20), `NUGGETS & NOTES` (18), `DISCHARGE BALTIMORE` (16), `SUMMARY` (16), `GAMEPLAN` (15), `TABLE OF CONTENTS` (14), `ATTN BOB KLIDE CC MARTIN STANLEY` (14), `EQUITY LEASE FOR TERM OF 36 MONTHS` (14)

## Rules checked against these numbers

| Rule or hypothesis | Data | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Only parse the real header block | X-To is in 41,264 files; To is missing in 613 | keep |
| Every file has its own Message-ID | 41,264 files, 17,903 distinct fingerprints | keep: copies are matched by fingerprint |
| Bcc is a copy of Cc | 10,832 of 10,832 Bcc headers equal Cc exactly | keep: still stored, never scored |
| About 3% have no To: | 613 of 41,264 files (1.5%) | corrected: 1.5% of files here |
| Files use CRLF | 38,476 files mix CRLF headers with LF body lines | corrected: convert CRLF to LF, keep LF |
| A few need cp1252 | 11 cp1252, 0 latin-1 | keep |
| Content-Type always text/plain | text/plain; charset=us-ascii, text/plain; charset=ANSI_X3.4-1968 | keep, but 1,165 files are quoted-printable and must be decoded before splitting |
| Dates look like -0800 (PST) | 41,264 parse, 0 fail; 84 bogus 1979-12-31 placeholders | keep; bogus dates stay (they parse) and are called out |
| Outlook Original Message is a reply marker | 1,281 of 3,740 first-marker Outlook blocks have an FW: subject | changed: FW: subject makes the block forwarded_content |

| > quoting is line by line | 1,514 files have > lines at column 0, 80 more only after spaces | keep column 0 only (indented > is mostly ASCII art and nested jokes) |
